Capture station and audience viewer

The mini PC shows one thing: is audio reaching the server? The phone viewer is caption-first, with the newest line at the bottom and the draft line in muted ink. It follows the phone's language (Spanish here).

Stage screen and stream overlay

These never follow the UI theme. The stage screen is set for projectors: large text, two or three lines, and an optional second language underneath. The overlay is transparent and sits on the program feed in OBS or vMix.
